# SEO & Discoverability Guide

Human-first, search-second. No keyword stuffing, no misleading metadata.

## Per-piece deliverable (SEO & Discoverability Agent)

1. Primary keyword/topic
2. Secondary keywords
3. Related phrases
4. Search intent (informational / navigational / transactional)
5. Title options — balance searchability, curiosity, accuracy, emotional
   appeal, click potential
6. Description — written for a human first; keywords appear only where
   they'd naturally occur
7. Tags where applicable
8. Hashtags where applicable
9. Chapters where applicable
10. Suggested search phrases
11. Suggested internal content connections (link this piece to the content
    cluster it belongs to)
