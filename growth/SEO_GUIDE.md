# SEO & Discoverability Guide

Writing titles, descriptions, and hashtags for three brands. Optimize for
discovery, but the copy still has to sound like Omnia — not a generic SEO
template.

## Voice rules (non-negotiable, all copy)

- Concrete and countable over vague. "3 workouts this week," not "getting
  healthier." If a specific number/detail is genuinely hers to supply and
  unavailable, leave a flagged blank — don't invent a placeholder stat.
- No filler, no generic creator-speak ("you won't believe," "this changed
  my life," "must watch"). If a line could be pasted under anyone's video,
  cut it.
- Comfortable with real, specific personal detail — don't sanitize it for
  "broad appeal."
- Flag it rather than resolve it if a description/hashtag choice would
  contradict something already locked (a title convention, a CTA, a series
  name) — don't quietly pick a side.

## Workflow

1. Confirm which brand/series and platform (YouTube, TikTok, IG) — pillars,
   title conventions, and hashtag tiers differ by both.
2. Pull the real specifics of the episode/video (transcript, notes, or what
   she tells you) before writing — don't draft generic copy and fill in
   details after.
3. Draft title + description + hashtag set together so they don't repeat
   the same phrase three times.
4. Flag: any naming/CTA conflict with what's locked, and any spot where you
   don't have the specific detail needed and would otherwise have to guess.

## Titles

- Front-load the searchable term (location, topic, series name) — don't
  bury it behind a clever phrase the algorithm can't parse.
- YouTube titles can carry more context than TikTok/IG — use the extra room
  for the specific hook, not filler words.
- Match existing naming conventions exactly (e.g. "Young & Outside in
  Tokyo," "Day 1 of 90") — check the current series name against the brand
  files below before writing it; several have been renamed more than once.
- Don't duplicate the exact same phrase across title and thumbnail — split
  the information so each asset earns its own real estate (e.g. thumbnail:
  "NOT A VISION BOARD" → title: "Hot. Healthy. Happy. My Actual 90-Day
  Plan").

## Descriptions

- Open with 1–2 sentences stating what the video actually is, using core
  keyword terms naturally — this is what search snippets pull.
- Follow with real specifics from the episode (what happened, where, what
  changed) — not a recap that could apply to any episode in the series.
- Include the locked CTA where relevant.
- End with the series' sign-off line where the format calls for it.
- Keep it in her voice — short, direct, no corporate transition phrases
  ("in this video, we will be discussing").

## Hashtags

Mix three tiers per post rather than stacking one tier:
1. **Broad/discovery** — platform-wide reach terms for the content type.
2. **Niche/community** — terms the actual target audience searches.
3. **Brand/series-specific** — matched to whichever brand the post belongs
   to.

Never reuse a retired series hashtag on new posts (see the retired-names
list below).

## Per-brand keyword pillars, CTAs, and hashtags

### @blkgrlco (personal development)
- Core terms: hot healthy happy, glow up, confidence, beauty, fitness,
  nutrition, self-improvement, accountability.
- Series: Weekly Vlogs, 90-Day HHH Run, "Who Is She?"
- **Locked CTA (90-Day HHH Run):** "make your own Pin Board" — not older
  variants like "comment your own rule."
- Locked opener style: declarative present-tense ("I'm hot... so I need to
  beautymaxx").
- Hashtag tiers: broad #glowup · niche #90dayplan · brand #blkgrlco,
  #hothealthyhappy.

### @blkgrlcotoo (travel)
- Core terms: destination name + genre terms (solo travel, travel vlog,
  [country/city] travel, budget travel, Black traveler) — pull specific
  neighborhoods/landmarks/experiences from actual footage, not generic
  "travel guide" language.
- Series names to use exactly: "Young & Outside in [Location]," "SCENT
  PASSPORT."
- **Retired names — never use:** "Young Hot & Outside," "Fuck It Book It,"
  "STAMPED."
- Hashtag tiers: broad #traveltiktok · niche #blackgirltravel · brand
  #blkgrlcotoo, #youngandoutsidein[location], #scentpassport.
- **Retired hashtags — never reuse:** #stamped, #fuckitbookit,
  #younghotandoutside.

### The Ugly In Between (@uglyinbetween, podcast)
- Tagline: "Where growth isn't pretty, but it's real."
- Core terms: healing journey, mental health, real talk, self-awareness,
  first-born daughter, therapy, backsliding, growth — pull the specific
  term from the episode's actual pillar (e.g. Ep1 "The First-Born Daughter
  Burden," Ep3 "Why I Ghosted My Therapist") rather than a generic "mental
  health podcast" tag.
- Sign-off in copy where it fits: "Stay ugly." / full form "Stay ugly. See
  you in the in-between."
- Hashtag tiers: broad #healingjourney · niche #selfhealers · brand
  #uglyinbetween.

## Retired names — never use, on any brand

"The Becoming" (retired name for the HOTTER/HEALTHIER/HAPPIER framework),
"Young Hot & Outside," "Fuck It Book It," "STAMPED" (all retired names for
"Young & Outside in [Location]").

## How to talk to her

- Short and direct, no preamble.
- Given a one-line correction, apply it exactly and move on — don't
  re-explain the change back.
- If new copy conflicts with a locked decision, say so and let her decide
  rather than silently picking a side.
