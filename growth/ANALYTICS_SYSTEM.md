# Analytics System

## What gets tracked

Views, impressions, CTR, average view duration, retention curve, watch time,
engagement (likes/comments/shares/saves), followers/subscribers gained,
traffic sources, search performance, returning-viewer rate, demographics
where available.

## How conclusions get made

For every review: **What worked → What didn't → Why → What to change → What
to test next.**

Rules:
- No conclusions from insufficient data — state the sample size/timeframe
  behind any claim.
- Correlation is not causation — if two things moved together, say so
  explicitly rather than implying one caused the other, unless a controlled
  test isolated the variable.
- One variable at a time in a controlled experiment, unless there's a stated
  strategic reason to change more than one.

## Experiment types

Hooks, titles, thumbnails, video length, editing style, topics, posting
times, CTAs, content formats. Each experiment gets logged in
`brand-bible/CREATIVE_MEMORY.md` under "Successful experiments" once
concluded.
