# Analytics System

## Read this first: what analytics are *for* here

The studio-level goal is consistency, storytelling craft, and putting out
content she loves — not subs, watch-hours, or monetization (see
`../brand-bible/BRAND_BIBLE.md` §9; a monetization target was set and
scrapped the same day in 2026-09 for this reason). Analytics in this system
exist to answer **"is the craft improving and is this sustainable to keep
making,"** not "is this growing fast enough." Don't reintroduce
growth-chasing framing into reports or recommendations.

## Connected tools

- **Metricool** — connected to @blkgrlco (Instagram, Pinterest, TikTok,
  YouTube). This is the only brand with real posting history and real
  analytics right now (120 subs / 4 videos as of connection).
- **vidIQ** — connected to @blkgrlcotoo's YouTube (brand new, no content
  yet as of last check). Usable for general/competitor keyword research,
  trend data, and outliers — not yet for own-channel performance, since
  there's nothing to analyze.
- **@uglyinbetween** — no analytics tool connected yet.
- No live YouTube algorithm/analytics access exists anywhere in this
  toolkit. Research is grounded in live web search only — every report
  should say so rather than overclaiming insider access.

## What gets tracked (once there's real data)

Views, impressions, CTR, average view duration, retention curve, watch
time, engagement (likes/comments/shares/saves), followers/subscribers
gained, traffic sources, search performance, returning-viewer rate,
demographics where available.

## How conclusions get made

For every review: **What worked → What didn't → Why → What to change → What
to try next** — framed as craft feedback, not a growth mandate.

Rules:
- No conclusions from insufficient data — state the sample size/timeframe
  behind any claim. (4 videos and 28 minutes of total watch time is not
  enough to conclude much yet — say so plainly rather than overreading it.)
- Correlation is not causation.
- One variable at a time in a controlled experiment, unless there's a
  stated strategic reason to change more than one.

## Experiment types

Hooks, titles, thumbnails, video length, editing style, topics, posting
times, CTAs, content formats — framed as "what does she enjoy making and
does the audience engage with it," not as growth levers. Retention danger
zone to watch: ~75% mark (see `growth/CONTENT_STRATEGY.md` packaging
logic) — that's where a texture/cutaway beat belongs.

Each concluded experiment gets logged in `../brand-bible/CREATIVE_MEMORY.md`
under "Successful experiments."
