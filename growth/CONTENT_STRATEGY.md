# Content Strategy

Three brands, one studio: @blkgrlco (personal development), @blkgrlcotoo
(travel), @uglyinbetween (podcast). Confirm which brand before applying
anything below — pillars, cadence, and even the production rhythm differ by
brand. See `../brand-bible/brands/` for each brand's full pillar/series
detail; this file covers the cross-brand mechanics.

## Weekly production rhythm (@blkgrlco / @blkgrlcotoo)

- Saturday: batch film day.

  week's recap footage with new Sunday-only footage.
- @blkgrlcotoo is the exception: filmed only on vacation days/weekends off,
  never squeezed into the weekday rhythm.

@blkgrlco's daily shorts rotate by lane: Mon/Thu = HOT, Tue/Fri = HEALTHY,
Wed/Sat = HAPPY.

## Packaging logic

Synthesized from a Ludwig/Streamer University class plus current niche
research — not literal platform-algorithm access.

- Packaging (title/thumbnail or cover/caption) earns the click; retention
  earns the platform's continued push.
- Always A/B test title and cover — never ship a first draft.
- Specificity beats vibe — a number or named situation over a general
  topic.
- Series numbering compounds retention.
- Retention danger zone is around the 75% mark — that's where a
  texture/cutaway beat belongs.

Per-brand hook patterns (Idea Bank):
- **@blkgrlco:** Identity Call, Contrarian Strike, Open Loop, Confession,
  Results First, Mistake Warning, Direct Question, POV/Relatable.
- **@blkgrlcotoo:** compliment-getter recommendation, value/dupe
  comparison, fixable-mistake tip, mood-visual + specific detail.
- **@uglyinbetween:** contrarian/counterintuitive angle, concrete anecdote
  with real stakes, confession, debate moment, direct takeaway/question.

## The content ecosystem model

Generic repurposing toolkit — trim to the platforms actually active for the
brand in question (see Platform Strategy table below; none of the three
brands are on X/Twitter or Threads currently, and there's no newsletter).
One long-form piece should still generate a full family of derivative
content adapted to each destination platform's native behavior — never
copy/paste of the same asset everywhere.

```
ONE LONG-FORM VIDEO
 ├─ YouTube video (full)
 ├─ YouTube Shorts (1–3 cutdowns)
 ├─ TikTok (native hook/pacing, not a Shorts re-upload)
 ├─ Instagram Reel
 ├─ Instagram Carousel (key points as slides)
 ├─ Instagram Story (behind-the-scenes / poll / countdown)
 ├─ X/Twitter post (the single sharpest idea, as text or clip)
 ├─ Threads post (more conversational framing)
 ├─ Community post (poll/question to prime the audience pre- or post-launch)
 └─ Email/newsletter concept (deeper or more personal take)
```

For each derivative, the Repurposing Agent defines: hook, length, structure,
caption, CTA, editing style, text style, posting format. See
`agents/AGENTS.md` §8.

## Content funnel

```
DISCOVERY → INTEREST → TRUST → COMMUNITY → CONVERSION → LOYAL AUDIENCE
```

Every content idea gets tagged with its intended funnel stage before
production — a Discovery piece (built for reach/search) is judged on
different metrics than a Trust or Conversion piece.

## Content clusters

Group content by topic/pillar so that a winning piece has obvious,
plannable follow-ups rather than the next idea being random. When a piece
performs well, the immediate question is: "what 2–3 follow-ups does this
open up?"

## Content calendar framework

- **System of record: Notion** — Studios Hub (one page per channel),
  Content Calendar DB, Idea Bank DB. *"If it's not in Notion, it's not real
  yet."* Hub page ID: `3c77c3ea-6a8c-812b-8d56-e54b8ad3fa36`.
- Every scheduled item maps to: a brand, a pillar/lane, a platform, and a
  production step owner.
- **Calendar decisions optimize for consistency and creative quality, not
  performance-chasing** — see the Studio-Level Goal in
  `../brand-bible/BRAND_BIBLE.md` §9. A slow week that still ships
  something she's proud of is a better outcome than a rushed week chasing a
  metric.
- `growth/WEEKLY_REPORT_TEMPLATE.md` still tracks what's working, but as
  craft feedback for the Sunday "reflect" step — not as a growth-optimization
  driver.

## Automation vs. approval

Automate: research collection, topic/trend tracking, content organization,
SEO prep, repurposing drafts, analytics collection/reporting, calendar
updates, competitor monitoring.

Never automate without explicit authorization: publishing, brand
identity/visual changes, monetization/sponsorship decisions.

**Confirmed rule:** nothing auto-publishes for any brand. Every script,
edit, caption, thumbnail, and scheduled post — including anything queued in
Metricool — goes through Omnia for approval before it goes out.
