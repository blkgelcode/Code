# mexico-city-weekend — thumbnail concepts (words only)

**Stage:** concepts in words. No frames have been pulled and no images have been
made, because the footage is on Google Drive and this session can't reach it.
Every frame below is chosen from her log (`media/docs/_moments.json`) and has to
be checked against the graded footage before anything is built.

**Brand (open):** this assumes @blkgrlco, HAPPY lane, 90-Day HHH Run. If she
says @blkgrlcotoo instead, the fonts and palette below do not apply (that
brand uses TAN Memories + Perfectly Nineties and the navy/marigold palette),
so the concepts would need to be redone.

**Deliverables when a concept is chosen:** `media/out/mexico-city-weekend_thumb_1280x720.png`
(YouTube) and `media/out/mexico-city-weekend_cover_1080x1920.png` (Shorts/TikTok/Reels
cover). Both use one real graded frame (T1, nothing AI-made), with a 320 px-wide
check render for each (T3).

## Fixed for every concept

- **Fonts (T2):** headline in **Advercase Bold**, all caps. Secondary line in
  **Editor's Note Italic**, lowercase. No other fonts are used, and Editor's Hand
  is not used.
- **Measured from the font files:** Advercase is a wide face, with cap height at
  about 0.86 of the font size. A 120 px headline gives caps about 103 px tall.
  At the 320 px phone check that is about 26 px, which reads clearly. Editor's
  Note at 60 px shrinks to 15 px at 320 px. That is the smallest size that still
  reads, so the secondary line is **never set below 60 px**. If it doesn't read
  at 320 px, cut it rather than shrink it.
- **Palette (G3)** for text and any plate: `#F0DAC2` cream, `#2E1500` espresso,
  `#49001B` oxblood, `#8C0532` wine, `#EBB9BA` blush, `#A8B118` olive-chartreuse.
  No colors outside the palette are added. The frame keeps its real graded color
  (P4: skin stays natural, and the frame isn't pushed toward the palette).
- **Face centered** (STYLE thumbnail rule 4). The headline sits in a band above
  or below the face, never on it. The bottom-right ~220×80 px of the 1280×720
  stays clear of text, because YouTube's duration stamp covers that corner.
- **Word budget:** no more than 3 words in the headline and no more than 5 in the
  secondary line. There are no stickers, arrows, circles, borders or emoji.
- **This sets the format.** Winning patterns for thumbnails are `[none yet]` and
  correction #3 says to "pick one font, stick to it, format consistently". So
  whichever layout she picks becomes the @blkgrlco long-form template and should
  only change on purpose later.

## Title split (T4)

`copy/titles.md` did not exist when this was written, because packaging is
drafting it in parallel. **Packaging/QC needs to re-check this once titles land.**
How the hook gets split:
- If the title carries the **confidence / filming-in-front-of-people** angle,
  use Concept B or C, or change A's headline so it doesn't spend that phrase.
- If the title carries **the place and the trip** ("Mexico City", "solo
  weekend", "San Ángel"), all three concepts are clear. None of them puts
  "Mexico City" in the headline.
- verify.py flags T4 when 60% or more of the thumbnail's words longer than 3
  letters also appear in the titles. Each headline below has 1–2 such words, so
  a single shared word ("filming", "public", "earned", "pretty") is enough to
  trip it. Packaging should avoid those exact words in the title.

## Research notes

- **What's working / overused in solo travel and lifestyle vlogs right now:**
  stock-style landmark shots with tiny faces read as generic. Stock travel
  imagery is the most recognisable stock there is
  ([1of10](https://1of10.com/blog/youtube-thumbnail-trends-2026/),
  [thumbnailtest](https://thumbnailtest.com/guides/vlog-thumbnail-strategies/)).
  The open-mouth shock face is fading as a default
  ([miraflow](https://miraflow.ai/blog/youtube-thumbnail-trends-2026),
  [upstream](https://upstream.so/blog/open-mouths-and-youtube-thumbnails/)).
  Solo travel is crowded: YouTube reported 200M+ views on solo-travel videos in
  H1 2025 ([YouTube blog](https://blog.youtube/culture-and-trends/solo-travel/)).
  The overused moves to avoid are an airplane window with "SOLO TRIP!", a
  passport in hand, a landmark with a pointing arrow, and a "I went to ___ alone"
  shock face. These sources are mostly tool vendors, so treat them as
  directional only.
- **What that means here:** a real, close, readable face with a real focal
  object or real emotion, and a short line that opens a question. That is what
  all three concepts do.
- **Place accuracy:** the market's common name is **Bazar del Sábado** (Plaza
  San Jacinto, San Ángel, Saturdays only;
  [Afar](https://www.afar.com/places/el-bazaar-sabado-mexico-city),
  [mexicocity-guide](https://mexicocity-guide.com/attractions/bazarsabado/)).
  She says "El Bazar Sábado", which is fine in her voice, but if the name ever
  goes on a graphic it should be spelled with the accent. None of the concepts
  below put it on the thumbnail.

    the secondary line is written as a diary aside, not a sales line.

    slightly self-deprecating headline, not hype.
  - None of their thumbnails, layouts, faces or phrases are copied. "Aurelia
    Gray" and "Rachel McDermott" are unverified and are not used.

## Concept A: "the confession" (RECOMMENDED)

- **Source frame:** `DJI_20260926102402_0245_D.MP4`, **~0:11–0:28**, Green, the
  confession ("first time I've had my camera out… recording in front of
  people… a little bit daunting… I'm working on confidence"). Pull every 0.5 s
  across that span and choose the frame **between words**: mouth closed or a
  small half-smile, eyes on the lens. A mid-syllable mouth looks awkward small.
  If 0245 is too tight or busy, use `DJI_20260926102904_0247_D.MP4` ~1:00–1:06
  instead (bench, same outfit; drop the croissant from the frame).
- **Expression:** a little nervous, a little brave. A soft, closed-mouth smile
  or a "here goes" look. Not a shock face.
- **Background:** the real morning street/park, out of focus, with greenery and
  sidewalk. It should read as "outside, in a city, people around", not as a
  landmark.
- **Headline (Advercase Bold, caps):** **FILMING IN PUBLIC**. One line, about
  110–115 px (measures ~960–1000 px wide), centered, in the bottom band (cap
  baseline around y≈640 on 1280×720). Cream `#F0DAC2` over a soft espresso
  `#2E1500` gradient that rises about 35% from the bottom, at 60–70% opacity,
  so it reads on any background.
- **Secondary (Editor's Note Italic, lowercase):** *for the first time*. 60–64 px,
  blush `#EBB9BA`, centered directly above the headline.
- **Composition:** her face is centered in the upper two-thirds, with the eyes
  around the upper third line and some sky/trees above her head. The text band
  is below her chin and never crosses the face.
- **Emotional trigger:** recognition. Most of her audience (Black women 25–40,
  confidence lane) knows the feeling of being self-conscious filming in front
  of strangers. It's the video's real central topic (correction #4: one central
  topic).
- **Curiosity mechanism:** an open loop. Did she keep going? How did people
  react? Did it get easier? The video answers it with the burger stop ("not a
  fan of eating outside") and the yellow dress at night.
- **Honesty check:** her words in the log support it ("kind of the first time…
  recording in front of people"). This needs to be checked against the
  transcript.
- **Shorts cover (1080×1920):** the same frame cropped to 9:16 with the face in
  the upper half. The headline is stacked in two lines, **FILMING / IN PUBLIC**,
  at about 130 px (~590 px for the wider line), centered at about y≈1250–1500,
  above the bottom ~350 px that TikTok/Reels UI covers. The secondary line goes
  above it.
- **T4 risk:** this is the highest-risk concept if packaging's title uses the
  confidence angle. If the title says "filming in public", "in front of people"
  or "first time", the headline changes to **A LITTLE DAUNTING** (her own word)
  and the secondary line to *alone in a new city*. Before using "alone",
  confirm the trip was solo (the spec's central topic is a draft).

## Concept B: "the donuts"

- **Source frame:** `DJI_20260925192047_0238_D.MP4`, **~0:00–0:14**, Green ("oh
  my god look at these donuts… Jigglypuff, Pikachu, Charmander"). Choose a frame
  where her face and the open donut box are both in shot, with the box
  pushed toward the lens and her face just behind it, delighted.
- **Expression:** real delight. Eyes wide and a big smile, but not a
  manufactured scream face.
- **Background:** the apartment interior (the blue room). The blue isn't in the
  palette, but it's real and stays. Text sits on a plate, not straight on the
  blue, because wine and oxblood on blue are low contrast.
- **Headline (Advercase Bold, caps):** **I EARNED THESE**. One line at about
  120 px (~985 px wide), across the top band, cream `#F0DAC2` on an espresso
  `#2E1500` gradient from the top.
- **Secondary (Editor's Note Italic):** *no sleep, a 4-hour walk*. Set at 60 px
  (~420 px wide) in blush `#EBB9BA`, under the headline, top-center.
- **Composition:** her face is centered, with the donuts in the lower-center
  foreground, overlapping the bottom third and slightly off-center so they
  don't cover her chin.
- **Emotional trigger:** joy and reward, a little absurdity (Eni's honesty and
  humor). It's the HAPPY lane in one frame.
- **Curiosity mechanism:** an "earned how?" open loop. The secondary line hints
  at it (the standby flight, no sleep, walking around until 4 PM check-in) but
  doesn't say it.
- **Honesty check:** the log says "I didn't go to sleep last night" and "walk
  around for like four hours". She also slept 30–45 min on the plane, so "no
  sleep" is her own words, not literally zero. If she'd rather be literal, use
  *30 minutes of sleep*.
- **Flag (her call):** the donuts are licensed Pokémon characters. A real photo
  of food she bought is her footage, but **no character or brand names go in
  the text**, and if she'd rather not lead with someone else's IP, B is out.
- **Shorts cover:** face plus box centered in the upper-middle, with the
  headline stacked **I EARNED / THESE** at about 140 px above her head.
- **T4 risk:** low unless the title talks about donuts or the long day.

## Concept C: "the yellow dress"

- **Source frame:** `DJI_20260926210419_0254_D.MP4`, **~9:12–10:00**
  (552–600 s), Green, the yellow dress on. Choose a frame where she's facing the
  lens, still, and lit. Use only this span of that clip. Backup:
  `DJI_20260926215746_0256_D.MP4` ~0:00–0:16, the close "I look so pretty"
  face. Confirm the dress is in shot there, and note that this clip's audio is
  TV-heavy (picture only).
- **Expression:** confident and settled, a small smile or a look straight down
  the lens. This is the "after" of the confidence story.
- **Background:** the apartment at night, warm and dark. Darken it slightly in
  finishing so the yellow and her skin carry the frame.
- **Headline (Advercase Bold, caps):** **I LOOK SO PRETTY**. These are her words
  from 0256. One line at about 105–110 px (~950 px wide), bottom band, cream
  `#F0DAC2` on an espresso gradient. Alternatively, the headline is stacked
  **I LOOK / SO PRETTY** on the right if the frame leaves room. In that case
  the face moves slightly left and the frame is wide enough to still read as
  centered.
- **Secondary (Editor's Note Italic):** *night two*. 64 px, olive-chartreuse
  `#A8B118` works next to the yellow. Check the contrast at 320 px and fall
  back to blush if it fades.
- **Emotional trigger:** aspiration and self-love, matching the brand motto
  (Be Free, Be Kind, Be Well) and the HOT/HAPPY glow.
- **Curiosity mechanism:** weaker than A and B. It's a statement, not a
  question, so it relies on the glow and the color pop.
- **Shorts cover:** this is the strongest of the three as a vertical cover
  (full-length dress, vertical frame). Use C as the Shorts cover even if A is
  the YouTube thumbnail, provided the two thumbnails don't need to match
  (her call).
- **Privacy:** the frame must come only from 552–600 s of 0254. Don't use any
  other part of that clip for the thumbnail.

## Recommendation

**Concept A for the YouTube thumbnail**, because it carries the video's one
real topic, it's honest, and the open loop is resolved in the video. Then
**Concept C for the 1080×1920 Shorts cover**, because the dress is the
strongest vertical image and the "after" pairs with A's "before". Concept B is
the backup if A's face frames don't hold up small (talking-head frames often
don't), or if the title takes the confidence angle.

## Frames to grab if none of the logged faces hold up

The logged moments are mostly talking, held at arm's length or filmed walking.
If none of them gives a still, sharp, centered face that reads at 320 px, these
are the 2–3 stills to shoot (on the Pocket 3 or iPhone, vertical 4K so one frame
crops to both sizes, with the face at eye level and not looking down at the
lens):

1. **Bench, daytime, pink top + denim jacket** (A): sitting on a park bench by
   a fountain, camera at eye level about 1 m away, a soft closed-mouth smile
   into the lens, with people blurred in the background. Shoot 3 seconds still,
   then 3 seconds of a small laugh.
2. **Yellow dress, warm light** (C): standing by the balcony door or the blue
   wall at golden hour or under warm lamp light, waist-up, looking down the

3. **Hero object in hand** (B-style): her face behind the thing she's excited
   about (a donut, a croissant, a market find), with the object held toward the
   lens at chest height and both in focus. Shoot it 2–3 times with different
   expressions.

## Before building (any concept)

1. Brand confirmed (@blkgrlco vs @blkgrlcotoo).
2. Reference named (C12; the STYLE thumbnail workflow needs one).
3. Graded footage in `media/graded/` (P2 grade first, so thumbnails use graded
   frames).
4. `copy/titles.md` final, then re-check the split against it (T4).
5. Footage orientation confirmed. If it's vertical, the 1280×720 is a
   center-crop of a 9:16 frame and needs 4K source to stay sharp.
