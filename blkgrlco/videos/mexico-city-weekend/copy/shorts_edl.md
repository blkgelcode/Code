her logged note and is marked *(check against transcript)*. Exact
in-points inside a logged span are marked `~` and get set from the
word-level transcript (C3: find her first word, never cut from the span
start blindly; C4: cut ums, false starts and repeats inside the span).

  `media/out/mexico-city-weekend_shorts.mp4` + `.srt`, transcript to
  `media/transcripts/shorts.txt`. Not rendered at this stage.
- Length: **~1:45** main cut (under 3:00, S1). A **~1:28 trim path** is at

  The Everyday LUT (P2). iPhone `IMG_*.MOV` is not log, so it skips the
  conversion LUT (spec). Reframe after the LUT (P3). **If the brand turns
  out to be @blkgrlcotoo, the LUT step changes; hold until she confirms.**

  `_TEMP` rule doesn't apply. Rows marked *L-cut* lay her own line from
  the next on-camera clip under the B-roll before it. **Music: not picked

  Until then cuts land on her line ends and on a body movement (C6).
- Native audio muted on every clip her log marks MUSIC (restaurant/cafe
  music in the background) and on the night clips with the TV on.
- **No "Day N" overlay.** The series isn't confirmed as the 90-Day HHH Run,
  and the overlay style would have to match the Day1of90 reference

  straight count (Day 1 = 9/6) it's Fri = Day 20, Sat = Day 21, Sun =
