## Deviations and why
- **Runs ~1:45, not 60–90 s**: three days plus an uncut confession. The
  trim path gets to ~1:28 without touching either.
- **Hook is a flash-forward** (4:53 PM door line before the morning
  flight). It's her strongest Red line; the day backfills right after.
- **L-cuts**: her own lines laid under the B-roll before them (rows 2–3,
  24–26). Her words, reordered only.
- **No Day N overlay**: series and reference both unconfirmed.
- **Skincare routine left out**: C7 (2.5–3.5 s routine holds) conflicts
  with the Shorts ≤2 s B-roll cap. Used one outfit shot instead and
  flagged it; I didn't pick a side.
- Muted native audio on clips her log marks MUSIC and on the TV-on night clips.
