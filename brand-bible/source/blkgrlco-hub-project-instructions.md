## Editorial identity
Omnia identifies as a **Documentarian**. Core rule across every channel: **open mid-action, end cold, name the specific activity — never the vague vibe.** Authenticity and specificity over polish.

## Her actual shot-list framework
Shorthand: **EW** (Establishing Wide), **WS** (Wide Shot), **MS** (Medium Shot), **CU** (Close Up), **AM** (Action/Movement), **OS** (Overhead Shot), **ECU** (Extreme Close-Up).

Scene structure: Intro → Key Scene 1 through 6 → Closing thoughts/Outro. Each scene: FILMED checkbox, reference photo, B-ROLL IDEAS, SCRIPT lines tagged by shot type.

Pipeline: **Plan → Film → Edit → Upload → Reflect** (post-mortem: what worked, what to improve, next goals).

## Packaging logic the tools run on
Synthesized from a Ludwig/Streamer University class plus current niche research — not literal YouTube algorithm access (no tool has that):
- Packaging (title/thumbnail or cover/caption) earns the click; retention earns the platform's continued push.
- Always A/B test title and cover, never ship a first draft.
- Specificity beats vibe — a number or named situation over a general topic.
- Series numbering compounds retention.
- Retention danger zone is around the 75% mark — that's where a texture/cutaway beat belongs.

Per-channel hook patterns used in the Idea Bank / Research Desk:
- **@blkgrlco**: Identity Call, Contrarian Strike, Open Loop, Confession, Results First, Mistake Warning, Direct Question, POV/Relatable
- **@blkgrlcotoo**: compliment-getter recommendation, value/dupe comparison, fixable-mistake tip, mood-visual + specific detail
- **@uglyinbetween**: contrarian/counterintuitive angle, concrete anecdote with real stakes, confession, debate moment, direct takeaway/question
