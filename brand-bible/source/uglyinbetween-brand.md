# The Ugly In Between (@uglyinbetween) — Podcast (Brand Breakdown)
*Part of BLKGRLCO Studios. Pair with `BLKGRLCO-STUDIO-OVERVIEW.md` for shared context (creator bio, production reality, tools, voice rules, thumbnail workflow).*

---

## Identity
- Tagline: *"Where growth isn't pretty, but it's real."*
- Full sign-off: *"Stay ugly. See you in the in-between."* (Short form in copy where a sign-off fits: "Stay ugly.")
- Core thesis: going beyond the pretty surface of "having it together" into the ugliness of actually healing.

## Five Pillars
- Radical Permission to Be Messy
- Sacredness of Backsliding
- Joy Is Suspicious
- Community Over Counsel
- Beauty in Unfinished Endings

## Voice
- Friend-talking-to-a-friend, **not** therapist-voice, not over-literary/scripted — this is a deliberate correction/pull-back from the original Bible doc's more dramatic style.
- Voice for copy: friend talking to a friend about what she's going through — not therapist-toned, not authoritative, not overwritten/literary.

## Visual Identity
- Woman fully composed from the neck down, raw emotion visible on the face only — deliberately contrasted against one polished/glamorous "together" shot to visualize surface-vs-underneath tension.
- Black-and-white, high-contrast portraiture.
- Smudged/running dark makeup as the emotional-rawness motif; expressions ranging from crying to laughing-through-tears to introspective.
- Recurring icons: wilting flowers, cracked phone screens, half-erased journals.

## Brand Palette & Type
- Palette: deep aubergine `#3A2E39` + faded terracotta `#DDBEA9`.
- Type: Garamond Italic headlines / Helvetica Neue Light body.

## Production Approach
- Record in one take, keep voice cracks/sighs/pauses.
- Minimal-to-no music.
- Batch-record multiple episodes per session; same outfit for continuity across a batch.
- The **documentarian rule** applies fully here: keep pauses, sighs, dead air where they carry emotional truth. This brand is explicitly **exempt** from the talking-head explainer exception used elsewhere — do not cut dead silence the way explainer content does on the other two brands.

## Structure
- Episode 0 "Why Ugly?" — pilot, fully scripted, ~22 min.
- 12-episode Season 1 in 3 acts of 4.
- **Act 1 — "Navigating the Cracks":**
  - Ep1 — The First-Born Daughter Burden
  - Ep2 — Your Body Is Screaming
  - Ep3 — Why I Ghosted My Therapist
  - Ep4 — Self-Awareness Isn't Healing
- Full Bible doc (scripts/structure beyond this summary) lives in Google Drive as the source of truth.

## Editorial Frameworks That Apply Here
- The documentarian rule (see Production Approach above) is the governing rule for this brand.
- From the Narrative Cuts reference (Tim Runia): **Match Cut** and **Rhythm/Speed Shifts** are the two worth borrowing — a match cut between two emotionally linked moments could serve the "surface vs. underneath" thesis without breaking the documentarian rule. **Smash Cut** could work for a single deliberate gut-punch moment (e.g. into "Why I Ghosted My Therapist"), used sparingly.
- From the Graphics/SFX reference (Yale Jeannette): explicitly **not** a fit — the fast-cut, graphic-heavy pop-up/SFX style is the opposite of this brand's one-take/keep-the-pauses documentarian rule. That reference is intended for @blkgrlco/@blkgrlcotoo instead.

## SEO / Hashtags / Titles / Descriptions
**Core terms:** healing journey, mental health, real talk, self-awareness, first-born daughter, therapy, backsliding, growth — pull the specific term from the episode's actual pillar (e.g. Ep1 "The First-Born Daughter Burden," Ep3 "Why I Ghosted My Therapist") rather than a generic "mental health podcast" tag.

**Sign-off in copy where it fits:** "Stay ugly." / full form "Stay ugly. See you in the in-between."

**Hashtag tiers (mix 3 per post — don't stack one tier):**
1. Broad/discovery — e.g. #healingjourney
2. Niche/community — e.g. #selfhealers
3. Brand/series-specific — e.g. #uglyinbetween

**Descriptions:** open with 1–2 sentences stating what the episode actually is; follow with real specifics from the episode; end with the sign-off line where the format calls for it.

## Thumbnail Visual Identity
- Palette: `#3A2E39` deep aubergine, `#DDBEA9` faded terracotta.
- Headline font Garamond Italic, body Helvetica Neue Light.

- Recurring icons: wilting flowers, cracked phone screens, half-erased journals.
- If a trending thumbnail convention conflicts with this raw B&W aesthetic (e.g. a trending bright/saturated style), flag it and let her decide — don't quietly override the brand identity to chase what's trending.
