Here's a pasteable checklist for Claude Code as your editor, built from your Nov 11 reset plus the retention video. Anything marked (kept) is an older rule that still stands.

**1. Before the build**
- [ ] Read all footage and write the master footage doc.
- [ ] State the one story in one line: question → context → attempt → honest result → takeaway.
- [ ] Write the hook (real number or thing, said in the first 5 seconds) and check it matches the title's promise.
- [ ] Apply the D-Log M to Rec.709 LUT, then reframe with your face centered (kept).

**2. Edit list (sent to you before any render)**
- [ ] Each clip gets a one-line reason it's there, plus what changed from the clip before. If nothing changed, cut it.
- [ ] B-roll must show what you're saying. Remove decorative shots.
- [ ] Pop-ups and on-screen text land when you say it, not early.
- [ ] Punch-ins only on real reactions (kept).
- [ ] The honest result gets its full time, even a missed goal (kept).
- [ ] The takeaway answers the question the video opened with.

**3. Low-res preview review**
- [ ] First 5 seconds confirm the title and thumbnail promise.
- [ ] Every section has something the viewer is waiting for.
- [ ] No section repeats an idea already shown.
- [ ] Judged on curiosity, cut, clarity and connection, and only the best version is sent.

**4. House rules (kept)**
- [ ] No employer or job named or shown.
- [ ] Phone on screen only when essential.
- [ ] Skincare: no product labels, no sound effects.
- [ ] YouTube uses original stickers only, and TikTok/IG stickers come from your cue list.
- [ ] Poppins for @blkgrlco only.

**5. Before posting**
- [ ] You sign off.
- [ ] Every long-form has three thumbnails (real photos, never AI-looking) and three titles.
- [ ] Posting time and order are correct (3:00 PM CT, in numerical order).

I left out the old rules you replaced on Nov 11: the "Day N of 90" title format, the auto short-form per video, the forced Pin Board CTA, cutting all silence, and the 5-line story-map approval step. Tell me if any should stay.
