STORY AND GROWTH CHECKLIST

[ ] Keep every episode connected to my 90-day Hot, Happy, Healthy challenge.

[ ] Identify one reason a new viewer would care: a relatable situation, useful takeaway, or real question.

[ ] When activities repeat, vary the story angle, not my identity or routine. Never invent drama, dialogue, or results.

[ ] Prioritize a clear opening, understandable progression, my personality, and an honest payoff before adding effects.

[ ] Keep production manageable. Do not add extra posts, footage requirements, or complicated edits without a clear purpose.

[ ] Use actual audience feedback to suggest one specific experiment at a time. Distinguish observations from assumptions.

[ ] Do not overhaul the strategy after one low-performing post or treat low views as a judgment of my personality. Look for patterns across comparable posts.
