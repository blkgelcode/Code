# Yap batch 01 — filming sheet

For: the ideal viewer — tried the methods, couldn't stick, ready to commit, needs
someone to go along with. TikTok / Instagram.

**How to film:** one take per yap, phone or Pocket 3 vertical. Picture FaceTiming
*her* — someone who's quit on herself before. Don't redo takes for delivery;
I cut every pause and um. Say the hook first, then talk through the three
points in your own words. The points below are **prompts, not a script** — what
you say is yours.

**No methods, no tips.** You're not teaching her. You're showing her you did it.

---

## 1 — The 2pm reset

**Hook:** "I woke up at 2pm, my room looked like that, and I did it anyway."

1. What you actually wanted to do when you woke up (you said it on camera: get
   back in bed, close the curtains)
2. The promise you made yourself — "I'd do this even if I fail" — and when you
   made it
3. What you still didn't get done that day, and that you showed up the next
   morning anyway

## 2 — Doing it while not wanting to

**Hook:** "I don't want to do this. I'm doing it anyway."

1. The exact moment you almost didn't — where you were, what you were doing
2. The first small thing you did instead
3. How the day went after — honestly, including what you missed

## 3 — One off day, three misses

**Hook:** "On my off day I missed my wake-up, my workout and my night routine."

1. What happened — no excuses, just the day
2. What you told yourself that night
3. What you did differently the next morning (or didn't)

## 4 — The hour and a half that took four and a half

**Hook:** "I said meal prep would take an hour and a half. It took four and a half, and I didn't film it."

1. Why it ran over
2. The real reason you didn't film it
3. What was actually in the containers when you were done

## 5 — Why I'm filming every single day

**Hook:** "I've tried to change my life before. This time I'm filming every day, even the days I fail."

1. What you've tried before that didn't stick — name one and when you quit it
2. Why filming it, and showing the misses, is different for you
3. Where you are right now in the 90 days — the real count
