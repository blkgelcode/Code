# Ugly Duckling Studios: Brand File

Version 2, locked November 2026. Every graphic in every episode follows this file.
If something here changes, the change gets a new version number and a note at the bottom.

## The feeling

Warm, soft, honest, a little playful. A real beginner learning in public.
Humble, not shouty: that's why the wordmark is lowercase.

## Logos

All logos come as SVG (sharp at any size) and transparent PNG (3000 px on the long side),
in three colors: seashell, copper and burgundy.

| Logo | File name starts with | Use it for |
|---|---|---|
| Stacked: duck above "ugly duckling / studios" | `ugly-duckling-stacked-` | Opening title card, channel art, profile picture |
| Side by side: duck left, words right | `ugly-duckling-horizontal-` | End screens, wide short spaces |
| Words only | `ugly-duckling-wordmark-` | Small sign-offs, when the duck is already on screen |
| Duck with blush | `ugly-duckling-duck-` | Corner logo during talk-to-camera, small icons |
| Duck without blush | `ugly-duckling-duck-noblush-` | Anywhere only one color is allowed (stamps, embroidery, one-color print) |

**Which color:**
- Seashell logo on copper, or on darker footage. This is the main look.
- Copper logo on seashell, or on bright footage.
- Burgundy logo on pastel blue or seashell.
- Never put a copper logo on burgundy, or a burgundy logo on copper: the two are too close to read.

**Rules:**
- Don't stretch, rotate, outline, or add shadows to the logos.
- Leave clear space around a logo at least as wide as the duck's head.
- The corner duck (seashell, with blush) sits top right, about 110 px tall on a 1080p frame, at 70% opacity,
  and only during talk-to-camera moments. Never bottom right: YouTube's player buttons
  and your own channel watermark go there.

**Retired:** the old swash-U set (swash U, stepped "U G L Y", UD circles, the
"ugly reclaimed" badge, and the old wordmarks). Don't use them in new videos.

| Seashell | `#F1F1F1` | Logo and titles on copper; light background for lesson diagrams |

| Old Burgundy | `#43302E` | Text on pastel blue or seashell boxes; the duck's blush on copper versions |

**Pairs that read well on a phone** (contrast measured):
- Seashell on copper (5.9 to 1): the main pairing
- Burgundy on pastel blue (8.6 to 1): lower thirds
- Burgundy on seashell (10.9 to 1): lesson diagrams
- Copper on seashell (5.9 to 1)
- Pastel blue on copper (4.6 to 1): big titles only, never small text

**Pairs to avoid:** copper with burgundy (1.8 to 1), and any light color on another light color
(seashell, pastel blue).

**Retired colors:** Buttermilk and Butter Yellow (you didn't like the yellow), and Nebula
(it looks the same as Pastel Blue).

These colors are for graphics only. The look of your footage is handled separately in the color grade.

## Fonts

**Bricolage Grotesque**, free under the Open Font License (fine for YouTube, merch, anything).
The files are in `fonts/`. Install them on your Mac by double-clicking each one.

| Weight | Use | Size on a 1080p frame |
|---|---|---|
| ExtraBold | Episode titles, big title cards | 110 to 140 px |
| Bold | Series labels, chapter titles | 44 to 60 px, capitals with a little extra spacing |
| SemiBold | Lower thirds, on-screen notes, diagram labels | 44 to 52 px. Never smaller than 40 px |
| Medium | Longer text on lesson cards, used rarely | 40 to 48 px |

Never set on-screen text smaller than 40 px at 1080p (80 px at 4K). Anything smaller is hard to read on a phone.

## Layout

- Keep all text and logos at least 96 px in from the left and right edges, and 54 px in
  from the top and bottom (1080p). Double those numbers at 4K.
- Lower thirds sit bottom left: a pastel blue box with rounded corners, a seashell stripe on its
  left edge, burgundy text. Over footage, add a soft shadow under the box so it lifts off busy shots. Leave at least 36 px of space between the text and the box edge.
- One idea per graphic. If it needs two lines, it's probably two graphics.

## Motion

- In: 0.4 seconds, a gentle slide up of about 20 px plus a fade, slowing as it lands.
- Out: 0.3 seconds, a fade.
- A graphic appears when the idea is spoken, and leaves before it becomes clutter:
  3 seconds at least for anything with words, 6 seconds at most for a lower third.
- Same timing everywhere, so the channel feels like one voice.

## Voice on screen

Honest, warm, encouraging, never preachy. Plain words. Lowercase is fine and friendly.
Celebrate first tries rather than hiding them.

## Where things came from

- Colors: your moodboard (November 2026). You picked copper with seashell (option 5 of 6)
  and dropped the yellows.
- Font: chosen by you from 19 candidates.
- Duck: drawn shape by shape in code by your Claude editor (not an AI image generator),
  based on your original duck: bigger head, sparkle eye, copper blush.
- Wordmark: Bricolage Grotesque ExtraBold and Bold, with the letters turned into shapes so the
  logo looks right even on a computer without the font.
